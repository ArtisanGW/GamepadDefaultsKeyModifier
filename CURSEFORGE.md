# Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier

With the controller UI on, the top face button (Y on Xbox) opens your target's menu. With no target, it does nothing. Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier gives it something to do then.

[![Support me on Ko-fi](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=storage.ko-fi.com/cdn/kofi5.png&h=36)](https://ko-fi.com/artisangw)

What Y does with no target, your pick:
- Off: nothing, as the game does (the default)
- Quests & Map: opens or closes the quest log and map
- Bags: opens or closes your bags

Y stays the game's:
- with a target: it opens its menu
- with a window open
- while holding LT or RT, or in a vehicle: the skill on that bar's Y
- in combat: the game doesn't let addons follow LT and RT in a fight, so your skills always come first

Y shows the chosen action's icon while it does it. The windows open as the game's own, so they close without an "Interface action failed".

Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier, or /gpdk.

A vanilla addon: it needs no other addon.

## Goes well with
- [Gamepad Fast Map](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1827949): opens and closes the world map without its hitch.
- [Gamepad Bag Layout](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1828743): your bags' buttons together at the bottom of the bag window.
