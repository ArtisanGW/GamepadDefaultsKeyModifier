# Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier

On the controller UI, the top face button (Y on Xbox) opens your target's menu,
and does nothing when you have no target. This addon gives it something to do
then:

- **Off** keeps the game's own behavior (the default).
- **Quests & Map** opens or closes the quest log and map.
- **Bags** opens or closes your bags.

With a target, Y still opens its menu. The button stays the game's own: the
addon only adds to it after the game's click, with no key bindings of its own.
Settings in **Options > AddOns > Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier**, or `/gdkm`.
