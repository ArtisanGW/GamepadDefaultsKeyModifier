# Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier

## 1.0.0

- First release: with no target, the controller's top face button (Y on Xbox) opens the quest log and map, or your bags
- Off by default: pick what Y does in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Defaults Key Modifier, or /gpdk
- Y shows the chosen action's icon while it does it, in place of the game's magnifier
- The windows open as the game's own, so they close without an "Interface action failed"
- Y stays the game's with a target (its menu), with a window open, while holding LT or RT or in a vehicle (that bar's skill), and in combat
